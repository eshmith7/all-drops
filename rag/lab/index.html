The index—
How to read itREFERENCE
recall@k — of the five questions, how many had the correct chunk inside the top-k.
faithfulness — how many answers were actually grounded in that chunk. Recall can be perfect while this is zero.
RRF — fuses the dense and BM25 rankings by rank, not score: Σ 1/(60 + rank). k=60 is the near-universal default.
lost in the middle — Liu et al., 2023. Models read the start and the end of a long context and miss what sits between. Bigger windows did not fix it.
